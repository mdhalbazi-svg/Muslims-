# AdMob setup status

AdMob is **not integrated** in this initial project. No real or test ad IDs are embedded. Before integration:
1. Create the app in your own AdMob account.
2. Add the official Capacitor-compatible Google Mobile Ads plugin and verify its maintenance/compatibility.
3. Configure App ID in AndroidManifest and ad unit IDs through build-time configuration.
4. Implement consent management and privacy disclosures.
5. Use Google's test IDs during development only.
6. Keep ads away from Quran reading, prayer, supplications, and other worship flows.
